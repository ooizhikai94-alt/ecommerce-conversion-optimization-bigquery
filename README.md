# ecommerce-conversion-optimization-bigquery
A BigQuery-based clickstream analysis project investigating e-commerce funnel abandonment across device, acquisition channel, browser, operating system, and geography.
## Business Problem

An e-commerce business may have substantial website traffic but still experience significant loss between product discovery and completed purchase.

The key business questions for this analysis are:

1. Where is the largest funnel leakage?
2. Does conversion performance differ by device?
3. Do acquisition channels produce different conversion outcomes?
4. Are there specific device and traffic-source combinations that underperform?
5. Are certain mobile browser/OS combinations associated with unusually low conversion?
6. Do conversion patterns differ across countries?
7. Which segments should be prioritized for further CRO investigation?
